Embarrassingly, I've been struggling to finish my memorization of the 30th juz of the Holy Quran for almost 2 years, off and on. I came up with the idea of placing the first couple words of each ayah on a document that can trigger my memorization. I'm sure it's not the most unique idea ever, but I've found it to be useful trying to take my hifz to a higher level.

[Here's the link](/quran/juz-amma.html). It lists the opening word or two of every ayah across all 37 surahs in Juz 30, with the ayah numbers marked next to each one.
